Flood Evacuation Routing — How to use

🗺️
Explore the map
Buildings are coloured by flood exposure: red (drowned), orange (close to flood), yellow (at risk) and green (safe).
🏠
Select a route
Click any building on the map to set it as your origin. Then click another building to set the destination.
🚨
Emergency routing
After selecting an origin, click "Find nearest safe building" to automatically calculate the safest route to the nearest unaffected building.
🧭
Follow directions
Step-by-step navigation instructions appear in the left panel once the route is calculated.
⚙️
Layer controls
Use the toggles in the left panel to show or hide flood polygons, buildings, colour coding and flooded road segments.
